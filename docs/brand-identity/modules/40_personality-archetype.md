# Module 4: Personality & Archetype

## Raw
- PRODUCT.md: "Trustworthy, clear, organized. The app handles family money — it must feel reliable and unambiguous, not playful or decorative."
- "Functional over decorative" — every visual element earns its place
- "Speed over beauty" — the Treasurer's workflow comes first
- "Local-first reliability" — trust is built through consistency
- User said: "family is first" and "when everyone sees everything, family bonds strengthen"

## Synthesis

**Archetype: The Caregiver + The Guardian**
The fund takes care of family members in hard times (Caregiver), but does so with discipline, transparency, and seriousness (Guardian). It's warm in purpose, clean in execution.

**Personality dimensions (Aaker 5):**
1. **Sincerity:** High — family-first, transparent, honest
2. **Competence:** High — reliable, organized, works every time
3. **Excitement:** Low — not playful, flashy, or trendy
4. **Sophistication:** Medium — clean and well-made, but not luxurious
5. **Ruggedness:** Low — this is about care, not toughness

**If the brand were a person:**
A trusted elder sibling or family accountant — the one everyone trusts to keep things straight. They're warm but no-nonsense. When they say "it's taken care of," you don't need to ask again.
