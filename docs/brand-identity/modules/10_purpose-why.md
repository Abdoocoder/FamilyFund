# Module 1: Purpose / Why

## Raw
- Built to help family members during hard times
- Family is first — the core belief
- Replaces Excel because it wasn't good enough (error-prone, unclear, slow for 48 members)
- Transparency is the mechanism: when everyone sees everything, family bonds strengthen
- The app brings the family closer and creates love — shared responsibility builds unity
- Monthly collection must be doable in under 5 minutes, zero ambiguity about who paid

## Synthesis

**Core purpose:** Replace manual spreadsheets with a transparent, reliable system that turns financial solidarity into a source of family closeness.

**Why it matters:** The fund is not just about money — it's a practice of showing up for each other. The app makes that practice visible, trustworthy, and easy.

**Candidate formulations:**
1. "Family first — transparent solidarity for the people who matter most."
2. "When everyone sees everything, everyone shows up for each other."
3. "From Excel to unity — bringing the family closer through shared responsibility."

**Open questions:** None — topic feels saturated.
