# Module 5: Voice & Tone

## Raw
- Interface is entirely Arabic/RTL
- PRODUCT.md: no playful or decorative language — functional clarity
- The Treasurer needs unambiguous status labels: "تم" (paid), "—" (unpaid), "مراجعة" (pending)
- Tone should inspire trust, not excitement
- "Transparent by default" applies to language too — no jargon, no marketing fluff

## Synthesis

**Voice principles:**

1. **Clear over clever.** Say exactly what something is. No metaphors for navigation, no playful empty states.
2. **Direct over polite.** "تم بنجاح" (completed successfully), not "لقد تمت عملية الدفع بنجاح" (the payment process has been completed successfully).
3. **Respectful over casual.** Family context requires warmth, but financial data requires precision. No slang, no emojis.

**Tone by context:**
- **Empty states:** Informative, not apologetic. "لا يوجد دفعات لهذا الشهر" (No payments for this month), not "عذراً، لا توجد أي دفعات" (Sorry, there are no payments).
- **Errors:** Specific, not alarming. "تعذر الحفظ. يرجى المحاولة مرة أخرى" (Couldn't save. Please try again.)
- **Success:** Brief and satisfying. "تم التسجيل بنجاح" (Registered successfully).
- **Guidance:** Instructional, not condescending. "اختر العضو من القائمة" (Select member from list).

**Vocabulary to use:**
- محاسب (Treasurer) — the operator
- عضو (Member) — a family participant
- الجمعية (The fund) — the collective
- اشتراك (Subscription) — monthly contribution
- رصيد (Balance) — outstanding amount

**Vocabulary to avoid:**
- English loanwords where Arabic exists
- Marketing jargon (premium, exclusive, optimized)
- Financial alarm words (debt, owed, delinquent)
