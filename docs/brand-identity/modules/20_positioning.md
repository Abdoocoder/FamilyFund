# Module 2: Positioning

## Raw
- Product.read: "Two audiences sharing one interface: Treasurer (المحاسب) as primary operator, family members as read-only viewers"
- ~48 family members, monthly subscriptions in JOD
- Replaces manual Excel tracking
- Primary differentiator: radical transparency — every member sees everything
- Anti-references: SaaS-cream dashboards, consumer fintech, government portals, over-minimal designer dashboards

## Synthesis

**Positioning statement (Moore template):**
To Jordanian families running a صندوق الجمعية, Family Fund is a subscription management app that replaces messy spreadsheets with a transparent, 1-click payment matrix. Unlike Excel or generic tracking tools, it makes every member's status visible to everyone — turning financial solidarity into a source of trust and closeness.

**For whom:** Arabic-speaking families (specifically Jordanian) running a family fund with ~20-100 members. The Treasurer is the primary user; family members are secondary viewers.

**Category:** Family fund management (niche — no direct competitors in this space)

**What makes it unique:**
1. Radical transparency — every member sees all payments
2. Arabic/RTL-native (not an afterthought)
3. Built for the Treasurer's speed: 48 payments in under 5 minutes
4. Local-first reliability (no server dependency)

**Candidate taglines:**
1. "صندوق العائلة — لأن العائلة أولاً" (Family Fund — because family comes first)
2. "شفافية تجمعنا" (Transparency brings us together)
3. "للمحاسب. للعائلة. للجميع." (For the treasurer. For the family. For everyone.)
